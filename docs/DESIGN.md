# SMARAN AI — Design System

## Product Personality

Smaran should feel:

- calm
- trustworthy
- warm
- dignified
- accessible
- human
- reassuring
- modern

It must NOT feel:

- childish
- hospital-like
- game-heavy
- overly technical
- visually crowded
- stressful

---

## Visual Direction

Design Smaran as a premium modern HealthTech application.

Use:

- generous whitespace
- rounded cards
- soft elevation
- strong hierarchy
- large accessible typography
- simple illustrations
- calm visual transitions
- minimal screen density

---

## Patient UI Principle

One screen should normally contain one primary task.

Avoid displaying more than 3–5 major actions simultaneously.

---

## Core Colors

Primary:
Deep calming teal

Secondary:
Warm sage

Accent:
Soft amber

Background:
Warm off-white

Surface:
White

Success:
Gentle green

Warning:
Warm amber

Error:
Muted red

Text:
Dark charcoal

Avoid pure black where unnecessary.

---

## Typography

Patient:

Headings:
28–34px

Primary action:
20–24px

Body:
18–20px

Secondary:
16–18px

Caregiver screens may use slightly denser typography.

---

## Touch Targets

Minimum preferred patient touch target:

56px

Major patient cards:

72px+

---

## Buttons

Primary buttons must:

- have rounded corners
- contain clear action text
- use large type
- provide pressed state
- support accessibility labels

Avoid icon-only primary actions.

---

## Cognitive Game Feedback

Correct:

"Wonderful! ❤️"

"Well done!"

"You remembered it!"

Incorrect:

"Almost! Let's try once more."

"That's okay. Here's a little help."

Never use:

"FAILED"

"WRONG!"

"BAD SCORE"

---

## Motion

Animations should be:

- slow enough to understand
- subtle
- optional
- disabled with Reduced Motion

Avoid:

- flashing
- aggressive shaking
- rapid transitions
- unnecessary parallax

---

## Patient Navigation

Primary navigation should remain obvious.

Always provide:

- Home
- Back
- Voice instruction where relevant

Avoid hidden gestures.

---

## Caregiver UI

Caregiver dashboard may use:

- compact metric cards
- charts
- timelines
- activity history
- reminder completion
- alerts

Charts must never claim clinical diagnosis.

---

## Offline Design

Offline mode is a capability.

Display:

"Ready offline"

rather than:

"No Internet!"

If synchronization is pending:

"Saved safely. We'll sync when you're connected."

---

## Accessibility

Every patient-facing feature should consider:

- low vision
- reduced motor precision
- slower interaction
- reading difficulty
- language preference
- hearing support
- cognitive overload
